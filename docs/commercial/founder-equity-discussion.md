# Founder Equity Discussion

**Status:** Working discussion only. No equity agreement has been made.

**Participants:** Zain and Yasir

**Recorded:** 25 July 2026

This note preserves the founder-equity discussion separately from the RentCottage operating-cost budget. It is a negotiation starting point, not legal, tax or investment advice.

## Current working context

- Zain expects to carry most of the product management, technical delivery and day-to-day development, using artificial intelligence tools where appropriate.
- The working budget includes a USD 4,000 monthly development and management payment to Zain, separate from software subscriptions and other project expenses.
- Yasir is expected to provide funding, direction, Iraqi market knowledge, relationships and commercial access. His exact time, capital and operating commitments remain to be agreed.
- Zain expects continuing responsibility after the initial build rather than delivery of a fixed, contractor-style software package.
- The legal entity, company jurisdiction, share structure and ownership of the product intellectual property have not been decided.

## Current negotiation position

If Zain is genuinely the continuing product and technical co-founder:

- **Opening request:** 50%
- **Target range:** 40% to 50%
- **Provisional floor:** 30%

The 30% floor only remains defensible if the USD 4,000 payment becomes genuine market-rate compensation, Zain's post-launch responsibilities are substantially reduced, and Yasir contributes materially greater capital and full-time commercial work.

This position is conditional. It is not a settled valuation or agreement.

## Role comparison

| Actual role | Indicative equity position |
| --- | ---: |
| Fully paid contractor delivering a bounded build | 0% to 5% |
| Early founding engineer without continuing business responsibility | 10% to 20% |
| Product and technical co-founder with continuing responsibility | 40% to 50% |
| Primarily unpaid founder carrying product, technology and operations | 50% or more |

The relevant question is not who first proposed the idea. It is what each founder will contribute, risk and remain responsible for over the years required to build the company.

## Terms that must accompany any percentage

The percentage alone is not enough. A written founder agreement should resolve:

1. Which legal entity owns the marketplace, brand, source code, customer relationships and revenue.
2. Whether the USD 4,000 monthly payment is salary, director remuneration, a contractor fee or another form of compensation.
3. The duration, review conditions and funding dependency of that monthly payment.
4. Founder share vesting. The current discussion assumes four years with a one-year cliff for both founders, subject to legal and tax advice.
5. Assignment of existing and future intellectual property to the agreed company.
6. Board seats, voting rights and decisions that require both founders' approval.
7. What happens if either founder reduces their time, stops working, becomes unable to work or leaves.
8. Whether cash contributed by Yasir is share capital, a founder loan or another investment instrument.
9. Dilution, future investment and any employee option pool.
10. Access to company accounts, contracts, payment-provider records and the capitalisation table.
11. A deadlock process rather than using an unfair equity split merely as a tie-breaker.
12. Whether the percentage is measured on a fully diluted basis before or after any option pool.

## Questions to resolve with Yasir

1. What will each founder be accountable for during the first 12 to 24 months?
2. How many hours per week will each founder commit before and after launch?
3. How much capital will Yasir commit, over what period, and what happens when more money is required?
4. Is the USD 4,000 monthly development payment guaranteed, and for how long?
5. What work and operational responsibility does Yasir expect from Zain after launch?
6. Who will act as chief executive, who will lead product and technology, and how will deadlocks be resolved?
7. Which company will own the product, and where will that company be incorporated and managed?
8. Will founder cash be treated as loans that can be repaid, or will it purchase additional equity?
9. What conditions would justify changing the initial equity split?
10. What happens to vested and unvested shares if either founder leaves?

## Recommended next step

Zain should discuss the responsibilities, time commitments, funding commitment and USD 4,000 compensation with Yasir before negotiating the final percentage. Once those facts are agreed, a qualified adviser should document the shares, vesting, intellectual-property assignment, founder departures, decision rights and tax treatment before substantial development begins.

Do not rely on a verbal promise of future shares.
