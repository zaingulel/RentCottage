# RentCottage MVP Approved Ticket Plan

**Status:** Approved by delivery owner on 2 August 2026; pending client sign-off. Do not publish these tickets until the signed Product Agreement is received.

This plan is generated from the client Product Agreement, the internal delivery specification and `CONTEXT.md`. Ordinary product tickets are vertical slices. Foundation tickets exist only where a shared production concern must be proven independently.

| Draft | Ticket | Blocked by | Observable delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | Deploy a trilingual marketplace shell | None | A TypeScript and Next.js application runs on Cloudflare Workers, connects to Supabase in separated environments, switches Arabic, Sorani and English direction safely, and passes GitHub Actions and preview smoke checks. |
| D02 | Enforce role-safe customer, owner and administrator access | D01 | Customer phone access, owner phone access and administrator multi-factor access resolve to explicit roles; Supabase Row Level Security and denial tests prevent cross-role and cross-account data access; privileged sign-in is audited. |
| D03 | Prepare an owner application and first private cottage page | D02 | A prospective owner saves a private first cottage page and submits it with identity, authority-to-rent, licence or exemption and payout evidence; incomplete submissions are blocked, unauthorised document access fails, and authorised access is time-limited and audited. |
| D04 | Review and decide owner applications within the service target | D03 | Administrators see the submitted owner, first cottage page and timestamped evidence queue, request missing information, pause and resume the three-day target, approve or reject with reasons, and keep the cottage private until every required approval is complete. |
| D05 | Complete the first cottage and create additional private drafts | D04 | An approved owner continues the first private cottage page from the application and may create more, with photos, capacity, rooms, amenities, approximate public location, exact private location and house rules; incomplete drafts cannot be submitted for publication. |
| D06 | Translate, moderate and publish a cottage in all launch languages | D05 | Source text is preserved, automatic draft translations are generated through a replaceable contract, administrators review all three versions, and publication or later content changes become live atomically without exposing drafts. |
| D07 | Define safe daily shifts and a full-day option | D06 | An owner defines exactly two or three non-overlapping shifts, including cross-midnight times, and a full-day bundle; a newly published cottage starts with every shift closed. |
| D08 | Price and open future availability | D07 | An owner sets standard, weekday and date-specific prices for shifts and full-day bundles, opens selected inventory and adds privacy-safe private blocks; precedence and bundle conflicts are deterministic. |
| D09 | Show an owner calendar without rewriting commitments | D08 | The calendar distinguishes open shifts, private blocks, pending holds and confirmed bookings; schedule, price and availability changes cannot alter held or confirmed inventory. |
| D10 | Browse and search approved cottages across Iraq | D06, D08 | Anonymous customers search published cottages by governorate or area, date, shifts, party size and amenities, see only complete-period availability, and view a privacy-safe profile in any launch language. |
| D11 | Select shifts and receive an exact preserved quote | D08, D10 | A customer selects one or more shifts or full-day bundles across consecutive days, with continuous access between consecutive full-day bundles, and sees Booking Price, IQD 5,000 service fee and Customer Total; the complete schedule, content, prices, commission and terms are preserved as a Booking Snapshot. |
| D12 | Prove the payment contract and validate provider candidates | D01 | A provider-neutral simulator and contract tests cover authorisation, reservation, later capture, release, refunds, disputes, replay and lawful owner settlement; Qi Card is validated first and compared with ZainCash and AsiaPay using sandbox and written commercial evidence. No provider is selected without passing evidence. |
| D13 | Prevent overlapping holds under concurrent requests | D02, D08, D11 | PostgreSQL transactions and constraints create or reject one hold across the complete Booking Period; competing customers, full-day components and one customer's overlapping requests cannot double-book under concurrency tests. |
| D14 | Authorise the Customer Total and submit a booking request | D02, D11, D12, D13 | A verified customer enters party size and note, accepts preserved rules, receives any late-cancellation warning, authorises the full total and atomically creates one immutable request and hold; failed authorisation creates neither. |
| D15 | Let the owner decide and release unsuccessful requests | D14 | The owner receives a four-hour request with stable details, hidden customer contact and visible commission, then accepts or declines the complete request; decline, withdrawal and timeout release both authorisation and hold with clear statuses. |
| D16 | Capture payment and recover safely for 20 minutes | D12, D15 | Owner acceptance triggers automatic capture; success creates one confirmed booking, failure starts a 20-minute held recovery route, and expiry releases unpaid inventory; signed duplicate or out-of-order events cannot create duplicate charges or bookings. |
| D17 | Confirm a paid booking and release access details | D16 | After payment, both parties receive a unique reference, preserved booking details, exact directions and mutual contact details; confirmation never appears from owner acceptance alone. |
| D18 | Message safely before and after payment | D02, D06, D16 | A phone-verified customer starts a cottage-linked text conversation before requesting and continues it through the request and booking. Before payment, filtering covers Western, Arabic and Persian phone digits, common separators, country codes, emails, links and social handles, explains rejections and flags repeated bypass attempts. After payment it permits contact details; translated messages preserve the original, access remains restricted, and the conversation becomes read-only seven days after the booking. |
| D19 | Show booking history and send preparation reminders | D17 | Customers and owners see the correct pending, confirmed and past outcomes and receive a 24-hour reminder with only the details appropriate to the booking state. |
| D20 | Apply the shared cancellation and refund policy | D16, D17 | Customer cancellation at least 48 hours before the first shift automatically refunds the full Customer Total; later cancellation and no-show do not under the standard policy; owner or administrator cancellation refunds in full; administrators can record a full or partial exception with amount and reason, and provider outcomes are handled idempotently. |
| D21 | Complete bookings and record no-shows or incidents | D19, D20 | Eligible bookings complete after the Booking Period; authorised staff record restricted incidents and no-shows without confusing them with reviews or payment disputes; payout and review eligibility follow the final outcome. |
| D22 | Publish genuine reviews and one owner reply | D06, D21 | A customer leaves one rating with optional written text within 14 days of a completed paid booking, the owner leaves one reply, both reject contact details and external links, automatic translation preserves originals, and moderation hides rule-breaking content without erasing evidence. |
| D23 | Explain owner earnings and payout status | D16, D20, D21 | Owners see Booking Price, 10% commission, refund effect, expected net payout and paid status per booking plus simple totals; payout eligibility respects completion, late cancellation, refund, dispute and administrator holds. |
| D24 | Search records and operate queues from a basic administrator dashboard | D04, D16, D20, D21 | Administrators search customer and owner accounts and cottage profiles; see owner and content queues, requests, bookings, refunds, reviews and incidents with status and date filters; edit or hide cottage content with an audit reason; and keep verification documents restricted to authorised accounts. |
| D25 | Reconcile marketplace money and export basic records | D20, D23, D24 | Administrators see booking count, Booking Price, service fees, commission, refunds and owner payouts, can explain totals at booking level, and export filtered records for advisers or accounting. |
| D26 | Suspend or reactivate unsafe accounts and inventory without losing history | D09, D24 | Administrators suspend or reactivate customers, owners or cottages, new restricted activity stops, pending requests are resolved when affected, and confirmed bookings, payments, messages, reviews and audit history remain intact. |
| D27 | Integrate the approved production payment provider | D12, D16, D23 | The selected licensed provider passes the shared contract in its sandbox and production-like environment, processes signed webhooks idempotently, supports settlement and exposes operational failure states without an unpaid fallback. |
| D28 | Integrate and control the production translation service | D06 | The chosen service passes Arabic and Sorani native-review samples, labels generated text, preserves and displays originals when translation fails, accepts poor-translation reports, excludes verification documents, supports retries and moderation, and enforces request and monthly usage limits. |
| D29 | Integrate Iraqi phone verification and booking notifications | D02, D17 | The chosen suppliers prove Iraqi phone delivery, sender requirements and failure reporting; verification, request decisions, payment recovery and reminders work through provider-neutral adapters. |
| D30 | Integrate privacy-safe maps and location release | D05, D10, D17 | Public browsing uses approximate location, confirmed bookings reveal the exact pin and directions, supplier keys remain server-side, and billing alerts protect usage. |
| D31 | Harden the production runtime, data and deployment path | D03, D13, D27, D28, D29, D30 | Cloudflare `workerd` tests cover runtime-specific behaviour; migrations, secrets, private storage, backup and restore, monitoring, spend alerts, rollback and production smoke checks are directly verified. |
| D32 | Prove the complete mobile journey in all three languages | D18, D20, D22, D25, D31 | Playwright journeys cover owner application, publication, discovery, request, decision, capture recovery, cancellation, messaging, reviews and administration at mobile viewports in Arabic, Sorani and English, including right-to-left and accessibility checks. |
| D33 | Satisfy and record every public-launch gate | D26, D27, D28, D29, D30, D31, D32 | Launch evidence confirms ten approved real cottages, customer payment and fee research, licensed-provider capability, legal owner-document checklist and retention, approved terms, translation review, security checks and operational readiness. |
